# CreatorMarket PRD

## Chapter 19 --- Product Roadmap, Scaling Strategy & Long-Term Vision

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This chapter defines the phased delivery strategy, long-term vision, and
measurable objectives that will guide CreatorMarket from MVP to a
globally recognized digital products marketplace.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 19.1 Product Vision

CreatorMarket aims to become the preferred marketplace for discovering,
buying, and selling digital products while empowering creators with
tools to build sustainable businesses.

Core pillars:

-   Global marketplace
-   Creator success
-   Buyer trust
-   AI-native experiences
-   Operational excellence

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 19.2 Product Principles

-   Build for scale
-   Ship iteratively
-   Measure everything
-   Automate where practical
-   Maintain platform trust
-   Keep creators at the center

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 19.3 MVP Scope (Phase 1)

Features

-   Shopping cart

-   Orders

-   Reviews
-   Wallet
-   Basic payouts

# 19.7 Scaling Strategy

Target Growth

Stage 1

-   1,000 creators
-   10,000 products
-   50,000 buyers

Stage 2

-   10,000 creators
-   250,000 products
-   500,000 buyers

Stage 3

-   100,000 creators
-   2 million products
-   5 million buyers

Platform architecture must support horizontal scaling throughout all
stages.
