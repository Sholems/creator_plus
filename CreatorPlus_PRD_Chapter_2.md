# CreatorPlus PRD

## Chapter 2 --- Business Requirements & User Personas

> Working Name: **CreatorPlus**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2.1 Business Objectives

CreatorPlus is a global multi-vendor marketplace dedicated to digital
products. The platform enables creators to publish, license, market, and
sell digital assets while giving buyers a trusted destination for
discovery and secure purchases.

## Primary Business Goals

-   Build the largest discovery-first marketplace for digital products.
-   Help creators monetize globally.
-   Provide secure digital delivery and licensing.
-   Support individuals, businesses, schools, churches, agencies, and
    enterprises.
-   Become the default marketplace for AI assets, templates, code,
    education, and business resources.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2.2 Stakeholders

## Platform Owner

Responsible for business strategy, platform governance, pricing,
compliance, partnerships, and growth.

## Buyers

Purchase digital products for personal or commercial use.

## Sellers (Creators)

Create storefronts, upload products, manage pricing, fulfill support
requests, and receive payouts.

## Affiliates

Promote marketplace products and earn commissions.

## Moderators

Review products, enforce policies, and moderate reports.

## Finance Team

Manage commissions, refunds, payouts, reconciliation, and financial
reporting.

## Customer Support

Resolve disputes, answer inquiries, and assist buyers and sellers.

## Enterprise Customers

Organizations purchasing products for teams through centralized billing
and license management.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2.3 User Personas

## Designer

Sells Canva templates, Figma UI kits, presentations, and branding
assets.

### Goals

-   Earn recurring income
-   Reach new buyers
-   Build reputation

### Pain Points

-   Low visibility
-   Difficult payment collection
-   Piracy

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Developer

Sells code templates, APIs, boilerplates, themes, plugins, and SaaS
starter kits.

Goals

-   Build passive income
-   Sell product updates
-   Offer commercial licenses

Pain Points

-   Unauthorized redistribution
-   Manual licensing
-   Poor discovery

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Teacher

Sells lesson plans, worksheets, question banks, and educational
resources.

Goals

-   Reach schools worldwide
-   Generate additional income

Pain Points

-   Limited marketplaces
-   Copyright abuse

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Business Consultant

Sells business plans, contracts, proposal templates, SOPs, financial
models, and strategy documents.

Goals

-   Productize expertise
-   Build authority

Pain Points

-   Reliance on one-to-one consulting

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Buyer

Needs trusted products with clear licensing, instant downloads, and
reliable support.

Goals

-   Find quality resources quickly
-   Purchase safely
-   Receive updates

Pain Points

-   Fake products
-   Poor search
-   Unclear licenses

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2.4 User Journey

## Buyer Journey

1.  Discover product
2.  Search or browse
3.  Compare alternatives
4.  Read reviews
5.  Purchase
6.  Download instantly
7.  Receive updates
8.  Leave review
9.  Follow creator

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Seller Journey

1.  Register
2.  Verify account
3.  Create creator profile
4.  Upload product
5.  Submit for review
6.  Product approved
7.  Product published
8.  Receive sales
9.  Request payout
10. Grow through analytics

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2.5 Marketplace Principles

-   Discovery-first marketplace

-   Transparent commissions
-   Fast search
