# CreatorMarket PRD

## Chapter 10 --- Seller Platform & Creator Experience

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This chapter defines the end-to-end experience for creators---from
registration and verification through product publishing, analytics,
customer engagement, and payouts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10.1 Creator Philosophy

CreatorMarket is designed to help creators build long-term businesses,
not just upload files.

Core principles:

-   Easy onboarding
-   Powerful selling tools
-   Transparent earnings
-   Actionable analytics
-   Global reach
-   Ownership of creator brand
